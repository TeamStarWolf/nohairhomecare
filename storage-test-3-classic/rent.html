Step-by-Step Instructions on How to Rent a Unit

Step 1: Click "Rent Now" under the storage unit size you desire.

Step 2: Click the "Select Unit" drop-down box to see which units are available.*
*You can compare the units listed to the "Unit Layout" tab on the site to see which unit you will be renting.

Step 3: Once you select a unit, fill out your contact and billing info. This will create a personal account for you to log into to pay your rent.

Step 4: Sign our rental agreement contract.

Step 5:  You will receive a call, text or email with your gate code in order to access the facility. Congrats, you've successfully rented a unit at BKM Storage!
