The origin story

Two bald guys. Two college degrees. Zero desk jobs that stuck.

Here's the deal: we both went to college. Got the degrees. Framed them, even — professionally, with perfect 45° miters, because of course we did. But every summer, every weekend, every "hey can you help me move this load-bearing wall" text since we were teenagers, we've been in construction and labor. The side hustle never stopped. Eventually we looked at each other's shiny heads and admitted the truth: the tools are the career. The degrees just mean we do the math right and write estimates in complete sentences.

So that's the name. Two bald guys who care for homes. No Hair Home Care. The barber lost two customers; St. Louis gained a renovation crew. Toasted ravioli in the cooler, Cardinals on the radio, level reading dead zero — Tower Grove to Kirkwood to the Metro East.
