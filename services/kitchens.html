The kitchen: where we go full send.

A kitchen remodel is a contact sport with 14 trades in one room. Here's exactly how we run the play — no mystery, no “trust me bro” without the receipts.

How we actually do it

The Kitchen Game Plan

Every kitchen, same discipline. The jokes are free; the sequencing is sacred.

1

Design & measure

We laser-measure the room (twice — you know the rule), map every outlet, window, vent, and water line, then plan the layout around the work triangle: sink, stove, fridge. If the dishwasher door and the island are going to fight, we find out now, on paper, for free — not on day 12 with a crowbar.

2

Demolition (the fun part, done boring)

Water off, power off at the breaker and verified dead with a tester — every time, no exceptions, that's how everyone keeps their eyebrows. Floors get protected, dust barriers go up, and cabinets come out in pieces so we don't discover your plaster is load-bearing gossip. Everything hauled off same week.

Real talk: 1950s St. Louis houses hide surprises behind every wall. We budget for them up front so the “uh oh” call is rare.
3

Rough-in: plumbing, electrical, walls

Moving the sink? Adding an island outlet? This is when supply lines, drains, and circuits get re-run — permitted and inspected where the code says so, because we like our work legal and your insurance valid. Then insulation, drywall, and a finish so flat you could skim-read off it.

4

Cabinets: the credibility test

Cabinets are where you can tell if a crew is elite or just loud. We find the high point of the floor, snap level lines around the room, and shim every base cabinet to dead level — because a marble rolling off your counter is not a personality trait we allow. Uppers get screwed into studs, not hope.

5

Counters, tile & finishes

Counter templating happens after cabinets are set (never before — that's amateur hour). Backsplash tile gets laid out from the center so the cuts land even in the corners. Then hardware, plumbing fixtures, appliance install, caulk lines like pinstripes.

6

The punch list & the reveal

We walk the room with painter's tape and hand it to you: tag anything you don't love. We fix it, we clean like your in-laws are due at 6, and then you cook the inaugural meal. (Tradition says it's t-ravs. We don't make the rules.)

Degrees put to use: load calcs for the island you want to sit four people, cabinet layout drawings you can actually read, and a schedule in writing. College taught us spreadsheets; job sites taught us everything else.
The full damage report

Everything That's Involved

Everything a full kitchen touches. Yes, we do all of it. Yes, in this order.

  • Layout & design — work-triangle planning, island clearances, ADA options if you want them
  • Demo & disposal — controlled teardown, dust containment, dumpster logistics
  • Plumbing rough-in — sink/dishwasher/ice-maker lines, moving drains, shutoffs that actually shut off
  • Electrical — small-appliance circuits, GFCI protection, under-cabinet lighting, island outlets
  • Drywall & paint — patches to full re-rock, finish sanding, primer and two coats — cut lines crisp
  • Cabinet install — leveled, shimmed, scribed to walls that were never straight to begin with
  • Countertops — template coordination, sink cutouts, seam placement where you won't see it
  • Tile backsplash — centered layout, level lines, grout and seal — no lippage, ever
  • Flooring — LVP, tile, or hardwood tie-ins to the rest of the house
  • Appliance install — ranges, hoods, dishwashers, the fridge water line everyone forgets
  • Trim & caulk — shoe molding, filler panels, caulk lines like they were printed
  • Final clean — we vacuum inside the cabinets. Yes, inside.
