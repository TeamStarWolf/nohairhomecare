Mid America Storage Solutions Unit Interiors

Secure, Durable, and Convenient Storage

At Mid America Storage Solutions, we offer storage units built for reliability and peace of mind. Our units feature:

  • Strong Construction: Designed with high-quality materials to provide lasting durability and protection for your belongings.
  • Concrete Floors: Easy-to-clean and solid concrete flooring ensures a sturdy base for all types of storage needs.

Conveniently Located in Eldon

Our facility offers a prime location with easy access, situated at the city limits of Eldon on Highway 52. Here’s how to find us:

  • Minutes from Downtown Eldon: Just a short drive from the heart of the city and Highway 54.
  • Formerly Red's Antique Mall: The location has a rich history and is well-known in the area.
  • On the Left-Hand Side (Entering Eldon): As you approach the city, you’ll see our facility just after the city limit sign.
  • On the Right-Hand Side (Leaving Eldon): Heading out of Eldon, you’ll find us just past the city limit sign.

Our central location makes it easy for you to access your storage unit whenever needed.
